Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 — DRAFT, have counsel review before publishing

This is a starter template, not legal advice. Required for Chrome Web Store submission and Google's OAuth verification review — replace the placeholders and have it reviewed before going live.

What we access

GSCSuite requests OAuth access to your Google Search Console data (webmasters scope) solely to display it in the extension and to perform the actions you explicitly trigger (inspection, indexing requests, sitemap submission). We do not access Gmail, Drive, or any other Google data.

What we store

Your OAuth token is cached locally by Chrome's identity API and is not transmitted to our servers. Our license server stores only: your license key, email (for receipts), plan, and device IDs for seat counting. We do not store your Search Console data on our servers.

Third parties

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Search/indexing requests go directly from your browser to Google's and Bing's/IndexNow's servers.

Data deletion

Email hello@gscsuite.online to request deletion of your license/account records.
